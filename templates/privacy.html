Privacy notice

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This notice explains how BAMEX-CRUE, operating as Bamex Crue in Uganda, handles information submitted through this website. Please review it and get appropriate local legal advice before launch.

Information we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, request category, and message. Please do not send emergency requests or sensitive medical information through this site.

How we use information

We use inquiry details to respond to you, understand the service requested, and coordinate follow-up. If email notifications are enabled, inquiry details are also sent to our designated contact email through the configured email provider.

Storage and service providers

Inquiries are stored in the website's SQLite database and are accessible to authorized Bamex Crue administrators. The hosting provider and, when enabled, the email provider process information as needed to operate the service. The website also loads icons from a third-party content delivery network, which may receive technical connection data such as your IP address.

Retention and your choices

We keep inquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your inquiry by emailing kizyben47@gmail.com. We may need to retain some records where the law requires it.

Cookies and security

The public inquiry form does not require an account. The private administrator area uses a necessary, secure session cookie to keep authorized administrators signed in. We take reasonable steps to restrict access, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact BAMEX-CRUE at kizyben47@gmail.com.
